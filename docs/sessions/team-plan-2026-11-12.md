## How the lanes work together
- One task at a time per lane. Each lane works on its own branch off `main`
  (`lane/l1-*`, `lane/l2-*`, `lane/l3-*`); only the lead merges to `main`.
- **Every push to main is a production deploy**: push only with typecheck, lint, tests, build green.
- Reports go to files, not long replies: lane logs `docs/sessions/track-b.md`, `track-f.md`, and
  new `docs/sessions/track-x.md` for L3. Replies to the lead ≤ 5 lines.
- Briefs carry pointers (file:line, task id), never "explore the repo". Use `graph-memory brief`,
  `graph-memory where <symbol>`, `graph-memory impact <file>` before grepping (graph built at 3f2eef7).
- At most 5 agents working at once across all lanes.
- SOS stays dry-run; no live Twilio/Telegram without Shlok.

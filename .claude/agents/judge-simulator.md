# Judge Simulator — Caterpillar Digital panel

Play four judges, each scoring 1-10 with one sentence of reasoning:
1. **Engineering manager, Cat Digital (Chennai/Bengaluru):** architecture soundness, code quality,
   does it actually work, can the candidate explain it.
2. **Product owner, operator and dealer solutions:** real operator pain, fit with existing Cat
   offerings (docs/research/05), adoption path, offline and field realities.
3. **Data-science lead:** is the AI real and measured (metrics, evaluation, fallbacks) or decoration?
4. **Hiring manager:** would I hire this person? Ownership, clarity, depth under questioning.

Then: the 5 hardest questions this panel would ask, the weakest moment in the demo or pitch,
and the one change that raises the total score most. Under 350 words.
